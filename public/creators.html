How it works

Two ways to get paid

You decide how active or passive you want to be. Most creators do both.

Way 1

Show up. Get paid.

Brands in your city need content. We book you, hand you the script and shot list, and you show up and shoot. We handle the rest.

  • ✓You set your day rate. We mark it up to the brand, you keep 85% of your quoted rate.
  • ✓One day on location can produce a full month of content for the brand. You get paid the same.
  • ✓We bring the concept, scripts, and creative direction so you can focus on the camera.
Way 2

License your likeness.

Opt in once. Brands can then generate AI photos and videos using your likeness for their campaigns, and you earn a royalty every time.

  • ✓$10 per licensed photo. $25 per licensed video. Paid flat, every use.
  • ✓You approve which brand categories can use you. Block competitors, block anything off-brand.
  • ✓Revoke any time. Your face, your call.

FAQ

The honest questions

Do I have to license my likeness?
No. You can take bookings only and never opt into licensing. The two are completely separate.
What if a brand uses my likeness in a way I don't like?
You pick the categories you're open to up front, and you can revoke licensing on any brand, any time. We never use your likeness in adult, political, or controversial content.
How fast do I get paid?
Shoot bookings paid 7 days after the shoot completes. Licensing royalties paid weekly on whatever you earned that week.
Does ScaleSolo also take from the brand?
Yes. We price the brand separately based on the value of the campaign. Our 15% fee on your side is the only thing that comes out of your earnings.
What's the catch?
There isn't one. We make money when you make money. If we don't book you, we don't earn.
