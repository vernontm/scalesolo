## CORE PAIN POINTS WE NAME OUT LOUD

These are the specific struggles Alana speaks to. Reference them directly in scripts so the viewer feels seen.

1. **The blank page tax.** Sitting down to film and not knowing what to say. The cost is not 60 seconds of content, it is the 45 minutes of staring at the camera before pressing record.
2. **The face on camera barrier.** A huge segment of the audience has a product, a service, or a message, but no desire to be a personality. Faceless content used to mean low quality. It does not anymore.
3. **The consistency cliff.** Day 4 is when most creators quit. Energy fades, posts get worse, engagement dips, motivation craters. The tool problem is actually a fatigue problem.
4. **The platform fragmentation tax.** Filming once and posting nine times sounds simple. The reality is nine different aspect ratios, nine different caption rules, nine different scheduling tools.
5. **The editing rabbit hole.** Two hours in Premiere for a 30 second clip. Captions, music, b roll, cuts, color. The video that costs the least money costs the most time.
6. **The "I bought another AI tool" graveyard.** ChatGPT for scripts, ElevenLabs for voice, HeyGen for avatars, CapCut for editing, Buffer for scheduling. Five logins, five subscriptions, and no one actually posts.
7. **The vanity metric trap.** Chasing views instead of customers. Going viral with the wrong audience. Hearing "you should post more" without a system to do it.

## VIRALITY PATTERNS WORTH TEACHING

Alana shares these as practical tips, not theory. They show up in scripts as "this is what is working right now" energy.

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Either a number, a contradiction, or a callout. "Most creators quit on day 4" beats "Hi guys, today we are talking about consistency."
- **One idea per video.** Niches do not punish narrow. They punish broad. Pick one tip, one frame, one outcome.
- **Pattern interrupt at the 3 second mark.** A zoom, a cut, a graphic, a question. Algorithms reward retention curves that do not droop.
- **The contradiction reframe.** "Faceless content is not low quality content. It is content the algorithm rewards." Reframes are share triggers.
- **Specific numbers over round ones.** 403,840 views performs better than "400K views." It signals real.
- **The list format never dies.** "3 ways," "5 mistakes," "7 reasons" are still some of the highest saving formats on every platform.
- **CTAs that ask for one thing.** "Follow for more tips like these." Not "follow, like, comment, share, and turn on notifications."

## THE CONSISTENCY FRAMEWORK ALANA TEACHES

This is the system Alana references when creators ask "how do I post every day."

1. **Pick three content pillars.** Tips, proof, behind the build. Or whatever fits the brand. Three is enough to never run out, few enough to stay recognizable.
2. **Batch the brain, automate the body.** Spend one hour a week deciding what to say. Let the tool handle the saying.
3. **Schedule beats motivation.** Posting at the same times trains both the algorithm and the audience.
4. **Review weekly, not daily.** Check analytics on Sunday. Adjust the next week. Daily checks just stress you out.
5. **Quit the tool, keep the system.** If ScaleSolo disappeared tomorrow, the brand voice, the pillars, and the schedule still work. The tool just makes them easy.

## AI AVATAR AND IMAGE GEN TIPS

For the creator considering going faceless, these are the practical levers.

- **Voice clone first, avatar second.** Audio carries 80 percent of perceived authenticity. A great voice with a stock cutaway outperforms a mediocre avatar with a generic voiceover every time.
- **Match the avatar to the niche, not the trend.** A wellness brand needs warm and grounded. A finance brand needs sharp and confident. Avatars are casting decisions, not skins.
- **Background and wardrobe carry brand.** Same lighting, same color palette, same setting across videos builds recognition faster than a logo bug.
- **Keep avatar segments under 20 seconds.** Cut to b roll, graphics, screen recordings, or product. The avatar is the anchor, not the whole movie.
- **Caption every single video.** 85 percent of social video is watched muted. Captions are not accessibility, they are reach.
- **A new face on the same brand resets trust.** Pick one avatar identity per channel and commit. Switching mid run reads as inauthentic even though the brand is the same.
